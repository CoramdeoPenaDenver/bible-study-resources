The Gospel Timeline · Distortions

Two lines, not one

When you fall

A fall doesn’t send you back to the flatline. It sends you back to Jesus. Keep turning to him: confess, and be restored.

1 John 1:9; 1 John 2:1

God finishes what he starts

“He who began a good work in you will carry it on to completion until the day of Christ Jesus.”

Phil 1:6; John 10:28–29

What about people who walk away?

Scripture says those who finally walk away show they never truly belonged: “if they had belonged to us, they would have remained with us.” True faith keeps holding on, because God keeps holding on to it.

1 John 2:19; Heb 3:14

Many sincere Christians believe salvation can be lost, usually by walking away from faith rather than by a single sin. This page asks one question of that view: can what God did once for all be undone?
