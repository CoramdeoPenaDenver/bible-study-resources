Dead in sinA flatline. No spiritual life until God gives it.

Your standingOnce for all. Fixed at the cross; it never rises or falls.

Your growthEvery day. Real ups and downs, but always heading up.

Your futureForever. At death God closes the gap and glory begins; at Christ’s return it is complete.
